Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Taso Social is committed to user safety and prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). These standards apply to users, content, interactions, and activity associated with Taso Social.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

Users must not create, upload, share, request, promote, or distribute child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or use Taso Social to sexually exploit, groom, abuse, or endanger a minor. Prohibited conduct includes:

  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.
  • Grooming or attempts to manipulate minors for sexual purposes.
  • Creating, requesting, sharing, or distributing CSAM.
  • Sexual extortion or trafficking involving minors.
  • Using the service to facilitate the abuse or exploitation of children.

Violations may result in removal of content, restriction or termination of accounts, and reports to relevant authorities where required by law.

3. Reporting a Child Safety Concern

To report suspected child exploitation, CSAM, grooming, or other child-safety concerns involving Taso Social, contact our safety team using the email below. Include relevant details and links where safe and appropriate; do not download or forward suspected illegal material.

Taso Social Safety Team
Email: connect.taso@gmail.com

Where reporting tools are available within the app, users should also use those tools to report the relevant account or content.

4. Review and Enforcement

Taso Social will assess child-safety reports and take appropriate action where a violation is identified. Actions may include removing content, restricting or terminating accounts, preserving relevant information where legally appropriate, and cooperating with law-enforcement or child-protection authorities as required by applicable law.

5. Compliance with Child Safety Laws

Taso Social is committed to complying with applicable child-safety laws and legal reporting obligations. We will report confirmed CSAM to the appropriate authority, such as the relevant national reporting body, when required by applicable law, and cooperate with competent authorities.

6. Child Safety Point of Contact

Our designated point of contact for child-safety matters receives concerns and coordinates responses relating to these standards.

Child Safety Contact — Taso Social
Email: connect.taso@gmail.com

7. Policy Updates

We may update these standards to reflect changes in our service, safety practices, applicable law, or platform requirements. The current version will be published on this page.
